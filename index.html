Help & FAQ

Everything you need to know about Growth

What is Growth?
Growth is a freelance platform built for beginners and professionals alike. We charge only 5% commission, offer free client meetings, and actively promote new freelancers.
How do I get started?
Sign up with your Gmail account, complete your profile, and start browsing jobs or posting your services. It's completely free to join!
How much does it cost?
Growth is free to use. We only charge a 5% commission on completed projects. No subscription fees, no hidden costs.
How do I get paid?
Payment arrangements are made directly between client and freelancer. We recommend using secure payment methods like PayPal, bank transfer, or Wise.
How do I report a user?
Visit the user's profile and click the "Report" button. Our team will review all reports within 48 hours.
Can I change my account type?
Yes! You can switch between Client and Freelancer in your dashboard profile settings at any time.
Is my data safe?
Yes. We use industry-standard security measures and never sell your personal information. See our Privacy Policy for more details.
How do reviews work?
After working with someone, you can leave a review on their profile. Reviews help build trust and credibility on the platform.

Still have questions?

Reach out to our support team and we'll get back to you.
